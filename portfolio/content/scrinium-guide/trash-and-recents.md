# Trash, Recents and new folders

## Trash

**Trash** is at the bottom of the sidebar. Drag items onto it, press **⌘⌫**, click the Trash button in the toolbar, or right-click and choose **Move to Trash**. The Trash button, like every toolbar button, can be moved or removed: choose **View → Customize Toolbar…** and drag items in or out.

Open Trash to see everything in it, including the Trash on external drives, with each item's **original location** and **date trashed**.

- **Put Back** returns items trashed from FileCabinet to their original folder. If that name is now taken, the item comes back with "copy" in its name. Items trashed in Finder don't record where they came from; use Finder's Put Back for those.
- **Empty Trash** asks for confirmation and is the only way to delete permanently.

Moving to the Trash can be undone from the Edit menu or the toast that appears.

## Recents

**Recents** is at the top of the sidebar. It lists files you opened, newest first, including files opened in other apps, plus anything opened through FileCabinet on any drive. Switch **Files / Folders** to see recently visited folders instead.

Right-click an item and choose **Remove from Recents** to hide it; the file isn't touched, and it returns if you open it again. Hide Recents entirely in **Settings → General**.

Files on drives Spotlight doesn't index appear only if you opened them through FileCabinet.

## New folders

Use the toolbar button, **File → New Folder** (**⇧⌘N**), or the right-click menu. The folder is named by the active [naming rule](../rename/naming-rules.md) and opens in rename.

**New Folder with Selection** (**⌃⌘N**, or right-click) moves the selected items into a new folder. Both are undoable.

## Appearance

**Settings → General → Appearance** switches between Light, Dark and System. The change applies immediately, and the terminal changes theme to match.
