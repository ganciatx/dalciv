## Favorites

Click a favorite to go there. To change the list:

- **Add a folder:** right-click it in the list and choose **Add to Sidebar**, or drag it onto the **Favorites** heading.
- **Remove:** right-click a favorite → **Remove from Sidebar**. (The folder itself isn't touched.)
- **Reorder:** drag a favorite up or down.
- **Open in a new tab:** right-click → **Open in New Tab**.

You can also **drop files onto a favorite** to move or copy them there. Dropped items move on the same disk and copy across disks; hold ⌥ to copy.

## Cloud

iCloud Drive and other cloud storage (such as Google Drive) appear here when they're set up on your Mac. They work like any folder. See Cloud folders.

## Devices

Your Mac's disk and any drives you connect. New drives appear automatically when mounted and disappear when ejected.

## Saved Filters

Searches you've saved appear here. Click one to run it. Right-click → **Remove** to delete it; drag to reorder. See Saved filters.
