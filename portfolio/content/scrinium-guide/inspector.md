Press **⌘I** (or click the inspector button) to show a panel on the right with details about your selection.

## What it shows

| Section | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Header** | The icon and name, or "N items" for a multiple selection |
| **Info** | One item selected: kind, size, dates modified and created, and where it is |
| **Tags** | Always. Edits all selected items |
| **Description** | One item selected |
| **Custom Icon** | One folder selected |

With **nothing selected**, the inspector shows the folder you're in.

For a folder, **Size** shows *Calculating…* briefly while Scrinium [scans it](../folders/counts-and-sizes.md).

## What you can do

- [Add and remove tags](tags.md), for one item or many
- [Write a description](descriptions.md)
- [Set or reset a folder icon](folder-icons.md)
- Select and copy the name, path and other details

The panel stays open as you move between items.
