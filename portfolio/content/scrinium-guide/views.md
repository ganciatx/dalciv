Pick a view from the toolbar's view picker, or press:

| View | Shortcut | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **List** | ⌘1 | Seeing details, sorting, counts and sizes, tags and descriptions |
| **Columns** | ⌘2 | Digging through nested folders |
| **Icons** | ⌘3 | Browsing images and documents by thumbnail |
| **Gallery** | ⌘4 | Flipping through photos or PDFs |

Each folder remembers its own view. See [Per-folder views](#per-folder-views).

## List

The list shows **Name**, **Date Modified**, **Size** and **Kind**, plus optional columns:

- **Items**, **All Items** and **Hidden**: folder counts
- **Tags** and **Description**: tags and descriptions

**Sort** by clicking a column header; click again to reverse. Folders always stay at the top.

**Show or hide columns** by right-clicking a column header, or use the [view options menu](#per-folder-views). **Resize** columns by dragging their edges. Your column choices are remembered for the folder.

## Columns

Click a folder to open its contents in the next column. Click a file to see a preview with its size and dates and an **Open** button. Double-click a folder to make it the new root of the window.

## Icons

Items appear as thumbnails (images and PDFs show a real preview). Click to select; ⌘-click to select more than one; double-click to open. Choose a size from **View options → Icon Size** (48 to 144 points).

## Gallery

A large preview of the selected item sits above a strip of thumbnails. Click a thumbnail to change what's shown; double-click to open.

## Hidden items

Hidden files (names starting with a dot, and items macOS marks hidden) are off by default. Turn them on with **⇧⌘.** or **View options → Show Hidden Items**. They appear dimmed. This is remembered per folder.

## Opening, copying and deleting

- **Open:** double-click, or right-click → **Open**. Folders open in the same tab; files open in their default app.
- **Copy and paste:** ⌘C, then ⌘V in the destination. Use **⌥⌘V** to *move* instead of copy.
- **New items:** right-click empty space for **New Folder**, **New Text File** or **New Markdown File**. New items are named by your naming rule.
- **Delete:** ⌘⌫ moves to the **Trash**. Scrinium has no permanent delete.
